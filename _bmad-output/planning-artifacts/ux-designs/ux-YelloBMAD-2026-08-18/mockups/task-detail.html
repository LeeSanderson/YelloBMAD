Three things in that right-hand panel are deliberate, and two of them came out of the review. First, her unsent text stays visible and selectable — she typed it, it is already on her screen, so showing it discloses nothing, and silently wiping it would be gratuitous. Second, focus moves to the banner, which is why it carries a focus ring: the editor was inerted while holding focus, and without a named destination focus drops to <body> and silently strands a keyboard user — on the one requirement the PRD says to judge the product on. REVIEW FIX Third, the announcement is assertive and is never throttled: there is at most one per Account per Space, and it is the single interruption that has earned the right to be one.

REVIEW FIX The right-hand case is the composition neither state covered alone. AD-9 pushes the lease invalidation — so it cannot reach her while she is offline. She keeps editing against her replica, believing she still has access, and learns only on reconnection when the frames are discarded rather than queued. It must resolve to “Access ended.” and not to a reconciliation failure, because her text was never admissible — it was not lost to a fault. And it must never show a sync-succeeded state first and then revoke it.
